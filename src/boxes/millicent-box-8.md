Millicent - Box 8

This box was made especially for my dear friend Millie. I met Millie way back in the early 90's when I first started working at Zabar's in Manhattan. We had many adventures together over the years - but most of all, she is one of the sweetest and kindest people I have ever met and I love her. 

Her husband Bernardo also works at Zabar's. He and I have a storied history as well. You can read a little bit about that in the poem [*Chaos and Consequences*]({{ "/poems/chaos-and-consequences/" | url }}).
