Red Diamond Series - Rubedo

These boxes began as scrap, offcuts of plywood headed for the bin. I cut them, joined them, put a torch to them, then stained them in layers until the color came up out of the grain. Every box in the Red Diamond (Rubedo) series carries a red diamond: the final reddening. Twelve were made. Keep something in it you’d be sorry to lose. Open one, and you’ll find its number and my Dedalus mark burned inside.
