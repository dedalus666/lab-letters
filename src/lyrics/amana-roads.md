Amana Roads – (sung to the tune of John Denver’s ‘Country Roads.’)

Almost heaven, Main Amana
Lily Pond and the Iowa river
Life is old here, older than the trees
Younger than the mountains, 7 Colonies

Gravel roads, take me home
To the place I belong
West Amana, East, High and Middle
Take me home, country roads

Dandelion wine, among a hundred others,
Farmer’s markets, Zuber’s bed and breakfast
Oktoberfest – yes – and Maifest in the spring
Misty taste of Millstream, beer that helps me sing

Country roads, take me home
To the place I belong
Main Amana, South and Homestead
Take me home, country roads

I hear her guitars on Friday nights she calls me
Where’s the open mic? Robert please put me on the list
Halfway through the set I get the feeling that I should have practiced yesterday
And today

Country roads, take me home
To the place I belong
7 Villages, here to choose from
Take me home, country roads

—joe Wilford, 2015
