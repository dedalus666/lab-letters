The flowers sway – a teardrop falls
And quiet echoes never scream
I reach for you I search for you 
For only you can pull me through this darkness<br>
An iron gate slams behind me 
Scarlet clouds stand above me 
A thirst for what I never tasted
A chance to breathe a chance I wasted<br>
Climb with me soar with me
Hold me till I beg for mercy
Dream for me live for me
Smother me with your possession<br>
The fog lifts the curtain closes 
Snakes writhe in fields of clover
We both realize it’s never over
It’s just a change of scenery
