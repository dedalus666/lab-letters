<div class="lyric-block">
We are all reliving the dream
Close the door on the past<br>
Smile on my face and a song in my heart
Set yourself free
With the spirit of life<br>
The world wants to tie and bind you
Break it’s chains and tear down its walls<br>
This lifetime we are going to make it
Get that golden ring on the merry go round
Keep listening—the song in your heart
Will tell you who you really are<br>
Then with simply trust and happy confidence
Go forth and fulfill yourselves, each other
And the spirit of life itself
