Her name was Jaxx!
J-A-double X, exclamation
The punctuation mark
a point of declaration

Hidden in the folds of her skirt
were many books
card tricks and sleights
and a parliament of rooks

Rumor told of the tarot
tattooed upon her breast
Queen of Swords and the veil
of the High-Priestess

The cognoscenti of the town
hailed Jaxx! a student of the White Witch
Whose body she had found
Deep in the bog of a dank ditch

Vengeance off her tongue
came forth a litany of spells
Cast as Jaxx! plunged a knife
and herself into a well

Her broken body never fetched
from the depths of the site
But they could feel her acid tears
In the Blood-Moon light

Was she a lover or a friend
Or her Mother, no one knew
But the curse of Jaxx!
Unleashed a darkness which grew

The ignorance of the town
crumbled the towers of truth
And they were robbed of
Peace, happiness and youth

To the elders that remained
A lesson dutifully passed
Save your judgement for the one
behind the cold, steel glass
