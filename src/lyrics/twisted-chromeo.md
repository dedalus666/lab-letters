<div class="lyric-block">
Armed with a backpack
Set to detonate
City streets beckon
Pull the wire and die<br>
One last prayer whispered at noon
A song of shrapnel—sung out loud<br>
Nails and screws watch guts and rusted car parts
Lime and staples, pens, bolts and silverware
Rings, chain-links, metal buttons and zippers
Bike spokes, barbed-wire and battery acid<br>
A passion for death like the love-struck Romeo
These are the guts of the twisted chromeo<br>
Corkscrews, sheet metal, drill bits and paper clips
Razor blades, glass, ball bearings and bottle caps
Radio antennae, cogs, rivets and keys
Door hinges, pipe cleaner, salt, sand and padlocks<br>
Martyrdom calling two-fisted blows
Cutting down lives with the twisted chromeo<br>
Belt buckles, brick chips, triple A’s and money clips
Coins, gears and quartz rocks, bracelets and lighters
Jacks, steel washers and circuitry switches
Ratchets and sockets, springs, needles and tacks<br>
White eyes in panic terror-stricken they blow
Down like weeds in the wake of the twisted chromeo
</div>
