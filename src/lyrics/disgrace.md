<div class="lyric-block">
There was a church on the corner down in East L.A.
Where the Puerto Rican homeless used to go every day
Door always open – let the hungry in
Every man and child of life in sin<br>
The Preacher thought the money was a worthy cause
To feed a hungry man despite all his flaws
He planned to build a shelter one-day too
He felt it in his heart it was a good thing to do<br>
Oh Lord – Are you listening?<br>
The heads of the Church called him in one day
“The budget is getting tight, we’ll have to do away
With your little project feeding people like those
This town is big enough there’s other places they can go.” 
The Preacher blew his top, he’d never been an angry man
But their uncaring selfishness he could not understand
He questioned their beliefs for this was wrong without a doubt
They said “Just find yourself another Church, because we want you out.” <br>
You’re a disgrace—disgrace
It’s a disgrace—disgrace
There’s only one true Christian and he died on the cross…
</div>

<p><em>j. Wilford, 1991</em></p>
