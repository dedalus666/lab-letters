---
title: Unfurl My Soul
kind: lyric
tags: lyrics, music, Trailside Rangers, TSR
layout: post.njk
date: 1993-02-01
note: Written end to end around Faulkner's As I Lay Dying — the only Trailside Rangers song composed entirely about one book.
---
<div class="lyric-block">
Addie:
As I lay dying- descending into hell
I can hear them building the box
driving in the nails…<br>
You cannot bathe me of my sins-oh no
A matter of words just the same- salvation too<br>
Don't try to save me - you can only forestall what must be
Life is preparing to stay dead
no shining light to see<br>
Unfurl my soul…<br>
Darl:
I cannot love my mother- my mother is no more
death a constant familiar- life its sullen core<br>
She cannot be if she is was- oh no
her eyes like two flames just blown out
and I said, "Jewel, your mother is dead"<br>
The odyssey continues
her only wish to be fulfilled
their motives are in question here
despite what she has willed<br>
Unfurl my soul…
</div>
