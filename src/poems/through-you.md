---
title: Through You
kind: poem
tags: poems
layout: post.njk
date: 2005-12-31
---

<div class="poem-block">
There was something to it
The way your hands dashed and crossed each other
The scythe-like curve of your wrists 
You were sitting straight, a delicate foot on the pedals, head slightly inclined
Eyes held in a stoic stare of possession
Possessed by the keys your fingers found with a sensual precision
Over and over again they fell
An ivory map spread out before you
Worn thin from your touch<br>
The song did not burst or explode
It spilled
Bled out of you 
Like the hot pulse of blood from an open-wound
There was longing and despair
Resentment and resolve
The unspoken words became thread
In the tapestry of the music
Woven into the fabric of all of this
Their design
Worked by the loom of your hands
Into an epiphany
A release
A constant and complex series of notes
Not simply chosen—but delivered 
Through you
</div>
