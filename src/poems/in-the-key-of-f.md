<div class="poem-block">
Tom said: 
“Car horns honk in the key of F.” 
I laughed, but I believed him. <br>
I have a good ear. 
Tom’s ear was even better. 
He could identify the sound of each number
On a touch-tone phone. <br>
When I moved to the city
I called him. 
“Guess my new phone number,” I said, and dialed it. 
He identified all but 2 digits
Then corrected himself. <br>
I have a good ear. That always amazed me. <br>
Tom’s not around anymore. 
But I still want to test his theory
Someday 
In a car with my guitar. 
An F chord. 
Honk the horn. 
</div>
