---
title: This Time Around
kind: poem
tags: poems
layout: post.njk
date: 2005-09-19
---

<div class="poem-block">
I write with a pencil
Because I make a lot of mistakes
See, look at that first line up there
I had to re-write it
With a pen—I’d have had to scribble the whole line out
And start over<br>
Life is like writing with a pen
We all do a lot of scribbling out<br>
A pen can make a mess of a paper
With too many scribbles
Crossing out
X-ing out
Blacking out
Sometimes whole pages are removed
Ripped up and thrown away
But it’s fine
We just go on to the next one<br>
One day we’ll get it right. 
</div>
