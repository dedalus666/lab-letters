FEAR OF WINE- AN INTRODUCTORY GUIDE TO THE GRAPE
BY LESLIE BRENNER


My crimson reflection in the glass
does not show the despair
of the heart you’ve crushed, alas
my spirits are aware

Leave me alone- let me breathe
you say, decanting roughly
But your body is full, you have my seed
you cannot serve me so abruptly

You gave in to the waiters friend- I popped across the table
he stuck it in just like the rest, whenever he was able
He has no screwpulls I declare
the way he twists and turns and torque’s you
He brings you such a nice bouquet
but he only wants a corkscrew

So now you leave me aging here
you know I’m not a quitter
Should you not finish me tonight
tomorrow I’ll be bitter
