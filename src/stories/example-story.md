---
title: The Box That Wouldn't Close
kind: story
tags: stories

date: 2026-04-30
note: A short piece about a box that never quite closed right, and what it taught me about finishing things.
---
The lid fit everywhere except the one corner where I'd sanded too much,
chasing a knot that kept reappearing no matter how far I cut it back.

I decided, eventually, that the box was telling me something about
the difference between finishing a thing and it finishing you.
