About 5 years ago, I was captivated by a most curious novel written by English author Susanna Clarke entitled ‘Piranesi.’ Imagine a boundless labyrinth of infinite marble halls, thousands of statues, and an ocean imprisoned right inside the walls, that crashes through staircases and floods rooms – where a lone narrator catalogs the tides and speaks to birds, believing this surreal house is the entire universe. What would be a prison to most was a paradise to the protagonist, for he knew of no other world. This kind of imaginative storytelling appeals to me on every level. I loved it so much I forced myself to read the book at a slow, leisurely pace – so that I could prolong the experience. It’s thoroughly enjoyable and you should read it.

Admittedly, I had no idea who ‘Piranesi’ was. The novel gets its name as an allusion to 18th-century Italian artist Giovanni Battista Piranesi, famous for his series of 16 prints titled *Carceri d’invenzione* (Imaginary Prisons). Giovanni Battista Piranesi created imaginary prisons – impossible architectures of stone and shadow, stairways that lead nowhere, vaults without ceilings.

All of this information was, to say the least, inspiring. I set about writing a collection of songs utilizing the imaginary prisons as a metaphor. The characters within find themselves trapped, navigating complex emotional labyrinths in a variety of scenarios and themes involving myth, magic, love, betrayal, and loss. The result is this collection of 5 songs:

* Curse of Jaxx! – A witch’s murder and her lover’s vengeance
* Rosabelle Believe – A love song and a code word whispered from beyond the grave
* Sky Woman – A woman of the skies, who created the earth on the shell of a tortoise
* Betrayer Moon – Tears of deceit and betrayal under a blue moon
* Curse of Pasiphae – The curse of a god and the wrath of a sorceress queen
